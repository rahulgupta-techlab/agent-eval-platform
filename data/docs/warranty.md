# Warranty

All gear, footwear and apparel come with a 2-year warranty against manufacturing defects.

Tents have a 5-year warranty on poles and frame. Fabric and zippers are covered by the standard 2-year warranty.

Headlamps and other electronic accessories have a 1-year warranty.

Normal wear and tear, misuse and damage from improper repairs are not covered.

To make a claim, email warranty@acme-outdoor.example with your order number and photos of the defect.
