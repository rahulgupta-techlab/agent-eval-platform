# Loyalty Program

Members earn 1 point for every 1 EUR spent on delivered orders. Cancelled and returned orders earn no points.

There are three tiers: Basic from sign-up, Silver from 500 points and Gold from 1500 points.

Silver members get 10% off accessories. Gold members get free express shipping and free returns.

Every 100 points can be redeemed for a 5 EUR voucher.

Points expire 24 months after they were earned.
