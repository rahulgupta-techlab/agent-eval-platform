# Shipping

We ship to all EU countries, the United Kingdom and Switzerland.

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days and costs 4.90 EUR. Standard shipping is free for orders over 75 EUR.

Express shipping takes 1-2 business days and costs 12.90 EUR, regardless of order value.

Orders to the United Kingdom and Switzerland may be subject to customs duties, which are paid by the recipient.

Orders placed before 14:00 CET on a business day leave the warehouse the same day.
